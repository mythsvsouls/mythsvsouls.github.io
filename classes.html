Choose your class

Eight classes, each with its own guide and trial in the Starter Village. Numbers show the level each skill can be learned.

Melee

Warrior

Frontline melee with high health and strong physical defence.

Skills: Fencing 7, Slaying 15, Thrusting 22, HalfMoon 26, ShoulderDash 30, TwinDrakeBlade 32, FlamingSword 35, CrossHalfMoon 38.

Magic

Wizard

Ranged elemental magic and powerful area spells.

Skills: FireBall 7, ThunderBolt 17, FireWall 24, MagicShield 31, IceStorm 35, FlameDisruptor 38, MeteorStrike 49.

Support

Taoist

Healing, protective buffs, poison and loyal summons.

Skills: Healing 7, Poisoning 14, SoulFireBall 18, SummonSkeleton 19, MassHealing 31, SummonShinsu 35.

Melee

Assassin

Fast melee strikes, stealth and poison techniques.

Skills: FatalSword 7, DoubleSlash 15, Haste 20, FlashDash 25, HeavenlySword 30, MoonLight 36, CrescentSlash 50.

Ranged

Archer

Ranged physical attacks, bows, traps and mobility.

Skills: Focus 7, StraightShot 9, DoubleShot 14, ElementalShot 20, BackStep 30, PoisonShot 40, NapalmShot 48.

Staff

Monk

Martial discipline and spiritual power with nine staff techniques. Male only.

Skills: Basic Staff Technique 1, Luohan Staff 20, Bodhidharma Strike, Diamond Staff, Dragon Subduing Staff, Heavenly Thunder Formation and Taunt at 48, Ten Step Strike and Luohan Formation at 60.

New class

Witch

Violet witchfire, a crone's curse, a circling broomguard and the three-burst Witches' Sabbath. Female only.

Skills: Witchfire 1, Crone's Breath 12, Broomguard 22, Witches' Sabbath 35.

New class

Shapeshifter

A nature caster who heals and summons, then takes another class's form for 60 seconds (one shared 3-minute cooldown). Keeps its own gear; health and mana keep their percentage when it changes.

Forms: Warrior Form 1, Assassin Form 15, Wizard Form 22, Archer Form 28. Each form lends that class's key skills.

Your first steps, whatever your class

  1. Village Guide - your welcome quest.
  2. Pet Keeper - a VillageHound joins you. It fights at your side and grows stronger as you hunt together.
  3. Your class guide - the class trial at the training ground north of the village. Pass it to earn your first class skill book.
  4. Kit Steward - your first class kit: a class weapon, a necklace, two bracelets, two rings, boots and a belt.
  5. Trial Steward - the road to Bichon Province, where the main story begins.

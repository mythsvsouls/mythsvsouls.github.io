The story

Long ago five orders sealed the frozen dark beneath Mir. Your journey starts in a small village and follows that seal, chapter by chapter, from the Border Village of Bichon to the souls of the Oma King.

  • Chapter 0 - Starter Village (level 1): Welcome to the village, A companion for the road, and your class trial.
  • Chapter 1 - Border Village (level 1): A cold welcome, Tea against the frost, The butcher's worry...
  • Chapter 3 - Bichon Road (level 10): Errands for the Wall, Oil for the gate, The lost jade ring...
  • Chapter 4 - The Dead Mines (level 18): Zombies in the mines, Bat wings, Danger in the cave...
  • Chapter 5 - Wooma Temple (level 19): Help the farmers, Medicine research, Boss of Woomyon...
  • Chapter 6 - Stone Tomb (level 21): Trip to Mongchon, The lost sword, The white boar...
  • Chapter 7 - Zuma's Shadow (level 30): Zuma rumours, The stolen history, The temple champion...
  • Chapter 8 - Red Valley (level 40): Deep into the woods, Wanted: the evil big ape, The red moon blade...
  • Chapter 9 - Oma Souls (level 52): Whispers of the souls, Crossbows in the dark, The Oma King, A thaw in Mir.
  • Chapter 10 - Evolution: the Evolution Trials.

Four story quests have an escort: a guard walks you through the streets to the place you need to reach.

The Underworld

Below Mir lies a second world. Nine towns give you shelter, and a chain of boss dungeons takes you from level 70 to 150.

The realms beyond In development

Past the Horned Throne, eight realms continue the journey from level 151 to 190. They are being built and tested now.

Level 151-153

Forgotten Dragon

Ashen Vale, Forgotten Citadel, Dragon Throne. Bosses: Ashen Matriarch, Forgotten Emperor, Ancient Dragon King.

Level 156-159

Shadow Emperor

Ashen Gate, Obsidian Crypt, Shadow Citadel, Throne of Eclipse. Bosses: Crypt Sovereign, Obsidian Matriarch, Shadow Emperor, Eclipse Avatar.

Level 161-165

Celestial War

Skyfall Pass, Sunspire Citadel, Abyssal Rift, Starvault Sanctum, Throne of the Eclipse. Bosses: Seraph of Ruin, Solar Justicar, Abyssal Sovereign, Astral Devourer, Eclipse Archon.

Level 167-170

Celestial Kingdom

Aurelian Reach to the Sanctum of First Light. Bosses: Seraphic Warden and Solarius, Fallen Star King.

Level 172-175

Dragon Empire: Ashen Covenant

Emberwind Pass, Cinderscale Nest, Ashen Foundry, Oathbreaker Vault, Cinder Gate. Bosses: Varruk, Oathbreaker Marshal and Rhazek, Ember Regent.

Level 176-180

Abyssal Kingdom

Drowned Causeway, Coral Catacombs, Sunken Citadel, Trench of Whispers, Abyssal Throne. Bosses: Reef Matriarch, Tidebreaker General, Sunken Regent, Whispering Colossus, Leviathan Sovereign.

Level 181-185

Infernal Realm

Cinder Approach, Obsidian Tunnels, Ashen Foundry, Magma Sanctum, Infernal Throne. Bosses: The Cinder Matriarch, Forge Tyrant, Molten Behemoth, Ashen Archon, Infernal Sovereign.

Level 186-190

Frozen Dominion

Frostgate Outpost, Glacial Pass, Icebound Crypt, Frozen Citadel, Dragon Sanctuary. Bosses: The Pale Huntsman, Crypt Queen Ysra, Frost Titan Korr, The Winter Regent, Vaelthyr the Frozen Dragon.

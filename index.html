What makes it different

A real start

You begin in the Starter Village with a hound companion that fights at your side, pass your class trial, earn your first class kit, then take the road to Bichon.

Two new classes

The Witch fights with witchfire, curses and Sabbath rituals. The Shapeshifter heals and summons, then takes Warrior, Assassin, Wizard or Archer form for 60 seconds.

Bosses that fight back

Bosses call you out by name, taunt you, unleash a burst when wounded and speak their last words. Realm bosses mark the ground before they strike, raise shields and summon guards.

A living world

Story scenes show the people who give you each quest, guards escort you through the streets, and townsfolk chat and greet you as you pass.

The Underworld

Nine Underworld towns and a chain of boss dungeons from level 70 to 150, each with its own chapter of quests.

Realms beyond

Eight new realms from level 151 to 190: dragons, drowned kingdoms, infernal forges and frozen citadels. In development
